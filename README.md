# FoodieVibe 🍜🌏

FoodieVibe is a community-driven web platform designed to connect people through food, recipes, and cultural storytelling.

Developed during the TikTok Ningbo Hackathon 2026, the platform encourages users to explore international cuisines, share recipes, discover cultural food traditions, and engage with a diverse community through an interactive and mobile-friendly experience.

---

## 🏆 Achievement

**TikTok Ningbo Hackathon 2026**

🏆 Most Popular Project Award (游园之星)

---

## 📖 Project Overview

Food plays an important role in cultural identity and social connection. FoodieVibe was designed to create a space where users can:

- Discover recipes from different cultures
- Learn the stories behind traditional dishes
- Share food experiences with others
- Explore cultural diversity through cuisine
- Access content through both web and mobile devices

The platform combines community interaction with educational storytelling to promote cross-cultural understanding.
