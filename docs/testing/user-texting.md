# User Testing

## Overview

FoodieVibe was demonstrated and tested during the TikTok Ningbo Hackathon 2026.

The objective of testing was to evaluate:

- Ease of navigation
- User engagement
- Mobile responsiveness
- Content accessibility
- Overall user experience

---

## Testing Participants

Testing was conducted with:

- Hackathon attendees
- Student participants
- Judges
- Event visitors

The final live demonstration was presented to an audience of more than 100 attendees.

---

## Testing Focus Areas

### Landing Page

Evaluated:

- First impressions
- Navigation clarity
- Understanding of platform purpose

### Community Features

Evaluated:

- Ease of content discovery
- Community engagement flow
- User interaction experience

### Recipe Experience

Evaluated:

- Readability of recipes
- Information organization
- Ease of browsing content

### Cultural Stories

Evaluated:

- Content accessibility
- Educational value
- User interest and engagement

### Mobile Experience

Evaluated:

- Responsive layouts
- Touch interactions
- Cross-device compatibility

---

## Key Feedback

### Positive Feedback

Users appreciated:

- Clean and modern visual design
- Intuitive navigation structure
- Unique focus on food and cultural connection
- Mobile-friendly experience

### Areas for Improvement

Users suggested:

- More interactive community features
- Additional recipe categories
- Enhanced search and filtering options
- Personalized content recommendations

---

## Improvements Implemented

Based on feedback received during development:

- Refined page layouts
- Improved navigation flow
- Enhanced responsive behavior
- Adjusted visual hierarchy for readability
- Improved consistency across pages

---

## Outcome

User feedback validated the concept of using food as a medium for cultural connection and community engagement.

The positive reception from attendees contributed to FoodieVibe receiving the:

🏆 TikTok Ningbo Hackathon 2026 — Most Popular Project Award (游园之星)
